About me

Hello! I’m Songtao, a Ph.D. student in Computing Science at the University of Alberta, advised by Prof. Xi Ye.

Previously, I received my M.Sc. in Computer Engineering from the National University of Singapore and my B.Eng. in Automation from Harbin Institute of Technology.

I study reasoning and adaptation in large language models, with a current focus on test-time training and continual learning. My broader interests include reinforcement learning and interpretability.
